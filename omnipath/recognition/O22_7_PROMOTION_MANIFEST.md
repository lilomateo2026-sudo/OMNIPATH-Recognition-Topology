# O22.7 Recognition Promotion Manifest Gate

Parent: O22.6 integration commit `ab1235e5cd90a9d327e7dca94464f35b71fdcde1`.

O22.7 adds a descendant promotion boundary. A recognition-topology candidate is promotable only when it references the frozen O21.3 constitutional root, preserves the canonical O22.2 through O22.6 checkpoint lineage, binds an exact O22.6 external attestation for the candidate commit, has zero unresolved contradictions, has zero unresolved escape specimens, and provides an immediate rollback commit.

The promotion decision is derived by the gate and must not be asserted by the candidate manifest itself. The candidate cannot authorize its own promotion. O22.7 does not rewrite O21.3, O21.4, or O22.1 through O22.6.
