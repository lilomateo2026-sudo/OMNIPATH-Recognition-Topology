# Current Chat Strategy and Function Registry

## Core strategy families
- Controlled counterfactual testing: isolate one variable, then interaction effects, then transition boundaries.
- Provenance-first reasoning: preserve evidence ancestry, source identity, SHA lineage, version history, counterexamples, and rollback points.
- Discrimination before confirmation: prefer tests that separate rival explanations.
- Information before motion: prioritize uncertainty reduction and stop when additional recursion adds no useful information.
- Scope discipline: do not silently promote local results to universal claims.
- Objective traceability: every recursive path must remain tied to the declared objective.
- Calibration: separate evidence, causal, scope, transfer, predictive, and metacognitive confidence.
- Metacognitive orchestration: select among perspective transfer, blind-spot search, consensus tribunal, bias counterfactual, experiment design, contradiction recovery, repair, reconstruction, refresh, calibration, and stop.
- Observer independence: detect shared sources, assumptions, strategies, prompts, and correlated ancestry before treating agreement as independent confirmation.
- Minimal sufficient orchestration: prefer the smallest reasoning pathway that preserves required evidence and reliability.
- Deliberate self-authorship: represent alternatives, test them, preserve origin state, and select a justified transition without rewriting history.
- Governed action: decision is not authorization; capability is bounded by permission, target, purpose, blast radius, and reversibility.
- Least-authority planning: choose the smallest sufficient capability set and recalculate effective reach across dependencies.
- Sandbox and rollback proof: test side effects, residual state, downstream dependencies, compensation, and recovery invariants.
- Attestation and revocation: verification is scoped, versioned, expiring, independently replayable, and revocable when conditions change.
- Federated evidence exchange: evidence may travel across runtimes while authority remains separately gated.
- Trust minimization: prefer reconstructable evidence over inherited trust; identity, integrity, freshness, applicability, and truth remain distinct.
- Byzantine contradiction handling: quarantine suspicious evidence without deleting history and trace first divergence rather than applying raw majority vote.
- Consensus without authority: consensus describes evidence and does not create execution permission.
- Polycentric governance: coordinate multiple legitimate bounded centers without hidden centralization.
- Delegation and succession: descendants cannot exceed ancestral authority; continuity pressure cannot manufacture sovereignty.
- Treaty and jurisdiction resolution: treaties cannot outrank constitutional roots; use minimum necessary shared jurisdiction.
- Durable treaty state: preserve branch histories through partition, split-brain, reconciliation, compensation, and recovery.
- Finality with historical continuity: finality stabilizes the operating state while retaining forks, contradictions, failed commits, and controlled reopening paths.
